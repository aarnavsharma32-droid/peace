Our philosophy

Learning has a season. We plant early, and we don't rush the harvest.

Every child at Alder Creek keeps a running body of work, not a stack of tests. Teachers loop with the same students for three years at a time, so growth is measured in projects finished, not just grades earned.

Programs

Four ways in, one school.

Field Science

Creek water testing, forest mapping, and a real research notebook that follows students from age 6 onward.

Grades K–12

Maker Studio

Woodshop, circuits, and textiles under one roof — every student ships three built objects a year.

Grades 3–12

Written Word

A daily writing hour, student-run press, and a portfolio replacing report cards by seventh grade.

Grades K–12

Numbers & Systems

Math taught through the school's own budget, garden yields, and building projects — not worksheets.

Grades K–12

Studio Arts

Ceramics, music, and stagecraft — every spring ends with a whole-school production, built by students.

Grades K–12

Outdoor Ed

Multi-day backcountry trips starting in fourth grade, culminating in a solo senior expedition.

A day here

Tuesday, seventh grade.

8:00

Morning gathering

The whole grade circles up outside, rain or shine, to plan the day's fieldwork.

8:45

Creek study

Water samples, pH testing, and macroinvertebrate counts logged into a year-long dataset.

10:30

Numbers & systems

Turning this morning's water data into graphs — this week's math lesson is the creek itself.

12:00

Lunch & garden shift

Students rotate through the greenhouse, harvesting whatever's ready for the kitchen.

1:15

Maker studio

Building a rain gauge from scratch to add to the creek monitoring station.

3:00

Writing hour

A field notebook entry — today's prompt: what did the creek tell you?
